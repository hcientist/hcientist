## 🔬 Research: HCI for the subtle stuff

I study **interpersonal interaction mediated by technology**: designing novel technological support for the subtle and delicate sociality of human relationships. A lot of that work happens in and around classrooms.

**Interests:** HCI · educational technologies · CS education · accessibility · assistive technologies · AI in CS education · AI in HCI · full-stack web · mobile
